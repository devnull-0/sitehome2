PW&i Studio

From the first sketch to the final trim. We build identities that hold together, then print them with care, on the paper, finish and format that suit the job.

  • Brand identity

    Logos, color palettes and type systems that work as well on a sign as on a business card.

  • Print collateral

    Business cards, flyers, brochures, menus, rack cards and catalogs, proofed before they go to press.

  • Large format & signage

    Posters, banners, window graphics, yard signs and trade-show displays that read from across the room.

  • Packaging & labels

    Product labels, boxes, sleeves and stickers designed to stand out on the shelf.

  • Stationery & events

    Letterhead, envelopes, invitations, programs and place cards for businesses and celebrations.

  • Prepress & file checks

    Color-checked proofs and press-ready files, even when you designed the piece yourself.

Hoffman Images

Light, patience and a good eye. Hoffman Images photographs people, products and places, in our own studio or out on location.

  • Portraits & headshots

    Professional headshots and personal portraits, with relaxed direction so you look like yourself.

  • Family & milestones

    Families, graduates, couples and the moments worth framing, in the studio or somewhere that matters to you.

  • Product & commercial

    Clean catalog shots and styled scenes for your shop, menu, website or next print run.

  • Events

    Openings, celebrations and company events, documented from start to finish.

  • Studio sessions

    A dedicated space with backdrops and controlled lighting, so every session starts ready.

  • Prints & wall art

    Your favorite frames printed in-house and finished, ready to hang or hand over.

The Studio: under one roof

The camera, the design desk and the printer are in the same building, so nothing gets lost between vendors. One conversation, one schedule and one consistent look from the first photo to the finished piece.

  1. Capture

    Hoffman Images photographs your people, products or space, in the studio or on site.

  2. Create

    PW&i Studio turns those images into a brand, a layout or a full campaign.

  3. Produce

    We proof, print and finish the work here, and check the color against the original.
