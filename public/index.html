High school senior photos in Washington

Senior year goes fast. Hoffman Images photographs high school seniors from across Washington State, in our studio or on location: city streets, the waterfront, a favorite park or your own neighborhood.

Bring your cap and gown, letterman jacket, instrument or best friend, and we’ll make senior portraits that look like you. When you’re ready, PW&i Studio can design and print your graduation announcements and party invitations from the same session.

Senior photo questions

When should I book senior photos?

Many seniors book in late summer or fall of senior year, and cap-and-gown sessions are popular in spring. If your school has a yearbook photo deadline, book a few weeks before it so there’s time to choose your favorites.

Where can we take senior pictures?

In our studio, or on location somewhere that feels like you: downtown, the waterfront, a park, your school or your own neighborhood. Share your ideas when you book and we’ll plan the session around them.

Can you print our graduation announcements?

Yes. PW&i Studio designs and prints graduation announcements, party invitations and thank-you cards using photos from your senior session.

Holiday photos for every celebration

Holiday photo questions

When should we book holiday photos?

About four to eight weeks before the holiday if you’d like prints or cards in time. Late fall is the busiest season, so book early for the winter holidays.

Do you photograph cultural and religious celebrations?

Yes. From Lunar New Year, Eid and Diwali to Hanukkah and Christmas, we photograph the holidays that matter to your family, with care and respect for your traditions.

Can you make our holiday cards?

Yes. PW&i Studio designs and prints holiday cards, invitations and photo gifts from your session.
